Our Mission

This project aims to explore the application of machine learning in predicting the compressive strength of concrete based on its mix design parameters. The main purpose is to reduce dependency on extensive laboratory experiments while maintaining reliable accuracy. Through data analysis and predictive modeling, we seek to understand the relationships between material properties and concrete performance. This system serves as an educational and research tool that demonstrates how artificial intelligence can support civil engineering innovations.
